    How to get the file
    1. On meso.watch, wait until your world's price table has loaded.
    2. Press ⌘S and save the page as Webpage, Complete.
    3. Pick that .html file with Update market prices, or several saved pages at once. The prices stay in this browser.

    Mark the shop's item list

    Open a player shop or hired merchant in the game, then drag a box over the item names and prices. Leave out the item icons and the scroll bar. You only need this if the scanner can't find the list on its own. A marked area stays put, so mark it again after resizing the game.

    If you shared your whole screen, the picture shows whatever was on screen at that moment. Press Grab in 5 s, switch to the game with a shop open, then come back here.
